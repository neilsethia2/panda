PANDA
Your anti-doomscrolling companion

INSTALL
1. Drag Panda into the Applications folder shown next to it.
2. Open Panda from Applications.

IF macOS SAYS IT CANNOT VERIFY PANDA
This copy is not notarised by Apple, so the first launch needs one approval:
1. Click Done on the warning.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the line about Panda and click Open Anyway.
You only do this once.

AFTER THAT
Panda appears in the menu bar at the top right of your screen, and a setup
screen walks you through the three steps to connect Chrome and Safari.

Everything Panda records stays on this Mac.
